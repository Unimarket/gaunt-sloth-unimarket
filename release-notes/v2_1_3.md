# v2.1.3

- The `Ctrl+G` slash-command menu now runs the highlighted command on Enter in terminals that send Enter as a linefeed.
- The `Ctrl+G` menu can be closed without side effects: press `Ctrl+G` again, press Backspace with nothing typed, or press `Esc`. With a slash menu open during a running turn, `Esc` now only closes the menu and the turn keeps going; a second `Esc` stops the turn.
